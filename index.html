OLLIE SKYWALKER

On the morning of his birthday, a secret message arrived from the Galactic Council.

After years of courage, kindness and legendary lightsaber practice, Ollie had finally qualified for the title he had been destined to earn:

OLLIE SKYWALKER.

But there was no time for cake just yet.

A squadron of Shadow Drones had stolen the Starlight Crystal, an ancient power source capable of opening a hidden gateway between worlds. If the drones reached the final planet, they could awaken the Dark Beacon and plunge the galaxy into chaos.

Only one newly qualified hero had the speed, skill and birthday-level bravery to stop them.

Ollie must travel across Aurelia, Volcara, Glacien and Noctis, defeat Darth Vader, Stormtroopers, R2-D2 units, TIE Fighters and Death Stars, recover the crystal fragments and reach the Dark Beacon before it activates.

The mission is secret. The danger is real.

And the galaxy is waiting for… Ollie Skywalker.
